RAMPSeg: a robust calibration-free image segmentation method for reliable melt pool analysis in laser powder bed fusion

Hasan, N., Lee, E. J., Wessman, A., & Shafae, M.

Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing, 2025

Dice score near 0.90 with a standard deviation of 0.04, running 11 times faster than Meta's SAM and 75 times faster than OpenAI's CLIPSeg. Outperforms thresholding, Otsu, SAM and CLIPSeg.

Why calibration-free matters

Melt pool segmentation methods usually need per-camera, per-machine calibration, which means the method a lab validates is not the method a shop floor can run. RAMPSeg removes that dependency, so the same pipeline transfers across builds and setups without retuning. The speed advantage over foundation models matters for the same practical reason: in-situ monitoring has to keep up with the build.

Improving sporadic demand forecasting using a modified k-nearest neighbor framework

Hasan, N., Ahmed, N., & Ali, S. M.

Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, 129, 107633, 2024

A zero-inclusive k-NN framework for sporadic and intermittent demand time series containing more than 30% zero observations, outperforming existing methods.

Algorithm engineering, not model selection

Standard forecasting methods assume a distribution that intermittent demand violates: long runs of zeros punctuated by irregular spikes. Rather than applying an off-the-shelf model and reporting the error, we modified the k-nearest neighbor algorithm itself so that zero observations carry information instead of being treated as noise or dropped.
